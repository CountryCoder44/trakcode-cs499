There's one especially handy slice pattern worth remembering:, text open bracket colon colon minus 1 close bracket, reverses a string.
The missing boundaries mean "use the whole string," and the minus 1 step means "move backward one character at a time." If, word equals code, then, word open bracket colon colon minus 1 close bracket, gives you, E. Dee. Oh. See.
We won't use any other negative step patterns in this topic.

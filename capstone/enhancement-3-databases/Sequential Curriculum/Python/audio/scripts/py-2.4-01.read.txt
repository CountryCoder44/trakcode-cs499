A text value like, hello, has the type string.
A string can hold letters, digits, spaces, and punctuation, Room 3B, is one string, and even, 42, in quotes is a string, not a number.
The, plus, operator joins, or concatenates, two strings end to end, so, cat plus dog, becomes, catdog.
Notice it adds no space on its own, you have to include one yourself, like, cat plus plus dog.
And, plus, only ever joins a string with another string.

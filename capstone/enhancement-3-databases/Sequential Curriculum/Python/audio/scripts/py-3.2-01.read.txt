Mix operators in one expression and Python needs a rule for what to do first, that rule is precedence.
double star, goes before, times, slash, double slash, and, percent sign, and all of those go before, plus, and, minus.
So, 2 plus 3 times 4, works out the multiplication first, 2 plus 12, then adds, giving 14.
(We won't chain multiple, double star, together in this lesson.)

Python is a high level, general purpose programming language. *High level* means you can work without managing a lot of machine level detail yourself. *General purpose* means it's suited to many different kinds of work, not just one.
It was first released publicly back in 1991, and it's used by total beginners and professional teams alike.
Python leans hard into readable code, but that doesn't mean the rules are optional, it still needs valid syntax.

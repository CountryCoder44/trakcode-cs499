You can think of a string as a row of characters.
A character might be a letter, digit, space, punctuation mark, or any other single piece of text.
An index is just the numbered position of one character in that row.
Python starts counting at 0, so, word open bracket 0 close bracket, gets you the first character.
If, word equals code, then, word open bracket 0 close bracket, is See and, word open bracket 1 close bracket, is Oh.
Each result you get back is still a string, just a one character one.

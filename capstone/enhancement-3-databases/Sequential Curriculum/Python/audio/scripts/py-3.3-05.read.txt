For ordering numbers, you've got, less than, greater than, less than or equal to, and, greater than or equal to, notice the equals sign always comes second, never first.
Both boundaries count as true:, 5 less than or equal to 5, and, 5 greater than or equal to 5, are both, True.
We're only comparing one pair of numbers at a time here, no chaining comparisons together yet.
